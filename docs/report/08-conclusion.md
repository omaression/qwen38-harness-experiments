# VII. Conclusion and Future Work

This report presented an evaluation harness that turns a change to an LLM feature into a paired, reproducible and explicitly specified promotion decision, enforced in CI without model calls. Scoring is grounded in execution, with hidden tests, values compared outside the process that runs the model's code, and a static scan that keeps unsafe patches from running. The gate applies a non-inferiority rule with an explicit tie policy and reports, with every decision, how large a change its suite could detect. Recorded replies make every report reproducible byte for byte, and a deliberately regressed candidate tests the gate in every CI run.

The two studies on a 27-billion-parameter model show that these properties are necessary rather than ornamental. In Experiment A an identity adapter was rejected by a margin-0 gate on the strength of one reply per split, a dev-split gain of +0.154 balanced accuracy could not be confirmed on a held-out split at ceiling, and six of the seven disagreements between arms were failures of format or truncation rather than of reasoning. In Experiment C a ternary model kept 71 of the bf16 model's 73 resolved held-out HumanEvalFix tasks (−0.027, McNemar p = 0.5) at 1.6 times the completion tokens, while an 8-bit engine control lost no bug-fix item. A second recording of the bf16 model alone changed one to three items per split and was rejected on both dev splits. On the demo suite the same arms were all at or near ceiling, and the gate's only rejections there were single format failures. At the suite sizes a product team can afford, the decision rule, its margin and the suite's difficulty determine the verdict as much as the change under test does. A margin near the measured noise floor, about 0.05 here, is the defensible setting.

Future work follows directly from the threats in Section VI:

1. **Calibrate the margin from a no-op arm, by rule.** Record a no-op comparison for every serving stack, as C0 did for the bf16 stack, and derive the margin, or a significance requirement on losses, from the measured discordance. Record one for the llama.cpp stack too, whose noise floor Experiment C did not measure.
2. **Separate format from capability.** Report functional failures and format failures as separate metrics, so that a regression in output format is not read as a regression in reasoning, and vice versa.
3. **Harder, larger and private suites.** Replace the ceiling-bound demo split and the contaminated HumanEval problems with a suite on which the baseline is far from 1.000, keep its held-out part private, give the judge wrong patches that do not reveal themselves by a guard, and extend the executor to repository-level tasks with per-task environments.
4. **Several samples per item.** Record several replies per item, or replies under several batch configurations, to estimate the nondeterministic component of each comparison, and evaluate batch-invariant inference [15] as a way to remove it.
5. **Price the tokens.** Add a cost gate, or a cost-adjusted primary metric, so that a candidate that is not worse but uses 1.6 times the tokens is a decision the gate states rather than a warning beside it.
